    How LeakedIn Works

    LeakedIn does not rely on a single fragile rule or a black-box AI. It executes an Intelligent 5-Tier Hybrid Pipeline:

    0
    AI Gatekeeper & Vision Filter: Intercepts non-recruitment submissions (recipes, candidate CVs, casual chats) to eliminate false positives before scoring.
    1
    Unicode & Script Normalizer: Strips zero-width exploits, homoglyphs (Cyrillic lookalikes), leetspeak, and resolves Indian phone & currency constructs with exact character mapping.
    2
    Calibrated ML Classifier: Isotonic-calibrated Logistic Regression with sublinear TF-IDF trained on EMSCAD and real-world Indian scam datasets. Provides linear-time XAI explainability.
    3
    Multilingual Heuristic Engine: 42 YAML-compiled regex patterns targeting Indian scam vectors in English, Hindi (Devanagari), Malayalam, and Hinglish/Manglish.
    4
    Domain, Identity & Threat DB: Damerau-Levenshtein typosquat checks against 100+ top employers, free-email spoof detection, and salted SHA-256 community blacklist lookups.
